About KOMBINER

KOMBINER is KADsuite's PDF app. It edits the page you actually have — scanned, signed, or exported from somewhere else — in place, on a fixed content surface with a real redaction step, real form and signature tools, and page-level operations. It does not route your document through a paragraph model or a vector canvas to do it; those are DOK's and KANVAS's jobs, and this file explains, in its own header comment, why that split holds. Export to KANVAS is offered as a one-way hand-off for illustration work, not as the native path.
